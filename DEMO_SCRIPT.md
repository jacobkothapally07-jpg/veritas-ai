# 🎬 3-Minute Video Demo Script for Hackathon Judges

> **Video Rule Check:** Must be under 3 minutes, showing the project running locally. Video quality does not affect judging. Can be hosted as an Unlisted YouTube link or public Google Drive link.

---

### Segment 1: The Problem & Pitch (0:00 - 0:35)
* **What to Show:** Screen showing the Veritas AI landing dashboard on `http://localhost:5173`.
* **What to Say:**  
  *"Hi everyone, this is Veritas AI, built for the SerpApi India Hackathon 2026 in the AI Agents track. In the tech industry, startups and public companies constantly make exaggerated claims—promising room-temperature superconductors, dendrite-free solid-state batteries, or fully autonomous humanoid robots. Investors and R&D leaders waste billions because they don't have time to audit every pitch against the actual legal patents and scientific literature. Veritas AI solves this by turning SerpApi's multi-engine search suite into an autonomous adversarial forensic auditor."*

---

### Segment 2: Running a High-Profile Audit (0:35 - 1:25)
* **What to Show:** Click on the **"QuantumScape Solid-State Battery"** scenario card (or click **"Execute Audit"**).
* **What to Say:**  
  *"Let's audit QuantumScape's commercial claims. Their marketing claims that their proprietary ceramic separator eliminates lithium dendrites and allows 15-minute 80% fast-charging with zero degradation.  
  Notice what happens when I click Execute Audit: Veritas AI doesn't just do a simple web search. In parallel, it queries four distinct SerpApi specialized engines:  
  1. SerpApi Google Patents — to check the legal claims and mechanical specifications filed with the patent office.  
  2. SerpApi Google Scholar — to audit peer-reviewed electrochemical literature and citations.  
  3. SerpApi Google News — to check short-seller reports, manufacturer scrap rates, and delivery schedules.  
  4. SerpApi Google Web — to pull engineering teardowns."*

---

### Segment 3: The Contradiction Matrix & Verdict (1:25 - 2:20)
* **What to Show:** Scroll down through the **Forensic Reality Score**, **Contradiction Matrix**, and **Timeline**.
* **What to Say:**  
  *"Look at the results:  
  First, Veritas AI gives an objective Reality Index of 28% vs 72% Marketing Hype, rating the technology at TRL 4 (Laboratory Validation).  
  Second, look at our Contradiction Matrix: while marketing claims dendrite elimination, Google Patents reveals Patent US11652230B2 requires a massive 3 to 5 atmosphere continuous mechanical clamp to stop void formation. Furthermore, Google Scholar papers from MIT and Stanford show dendrites still nucleate along ceramic grain boundaries at fast charging rates.  
  Third, in the Innovation Timeline, we can trace the exact trajectory from early 2018 academic papers, through 2021 patent filings, to recent 2026 OEM shipment delays."*

---

### Segment 4: Multi-Engine Evidence & One-Click Export (2:20 - 2:50)
* **What to Show:** Click **"Multi-Engine Evidence Explorer"** showing real patent IDs and Scholar links, then click **"Export Investor Dossier (.MD)"**.
* **What to Say:**  
  *"In the Evidence Explorer, judges and analysts can inspect every single patent disclosure, author citation count, and news article with direct verified links. Finally, with one click on 'Export Investor Dossier', Veritas AI compiles a complete confidential due-diligence report ready for investment committees.  
  By utilizing Google Patents, Scholar, News, and Web, Veritas AI turns SerpApi into an indispensable tool for truth verification. Thank you!"*
